    Query guide

    A few ways to explore

    quick brown
    Both terms, anywhere in a document.
    "quick brown fox"
    The complete phrase, in order.
    fo*
    Words beginning with “fo”.
    fox OR secret
    Either term. Use uppercase AND or OR.
    fox - dog
    Include fox, exclude dog. Keep spaces around the minus.
    Grammar and ranking details

    Boolean operations run left to right. Parentheses and mixed phrase/prefix expressions are unsupported. Lowercase “and” and “or” are ordinary terms.

    Scores sum normalised term frequency × smoothed inverse document frequency. They are relative to this collection, not probabilities. Repeated query terms contribute repeatedly.

    Matched-term counts cover the whole document. Phrase occurrences count complete matches separately. Snippets show up to three merged excerpts.

    Search is byte-oriented; full Unicode linguistic processing and stemming are not implemented.
